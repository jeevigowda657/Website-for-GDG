FAQ

Who can participate?

Any student, individually or in a team, from beginner to advanced.

Is there a registration fee?

No, registration is completely free.

What should I build?

A professional, responsive and interactive website for a meaningful purpose, with at least three meaningful features.

How do I submit?

Deploy your site publicly and share the link along with your GitHub repository.
